About us

A cake shop that started in one kitchen

Ziba Bakery began as a single home kitchen in Melbourne's inner north, baking birthday cakes for neighbours and friends of friends. The recipes haven't changed much since — we still mix, fill and ice every order by hand, in small batches, the same morning it's due to go out.

"Ziba" means beautiful in Persian, which is exactly what we set out to make: cakes that look and taste like someone made them for you on purpose, not off a factory line. This website is our first time taking orders online, so the kitchen can spend less time on the phone and more time at the bench.

How we got here

2019

Started baking birthday cakes from a home kitchen in Northcote, taking orders by word of mouth.

2021

Moved into a small commercial kitchen closer to the city, and added cupcakes and pastries to the menu.

2023

Started delivering across Melbourne suburbs, boxed in kraft paper and tied with string.

2026

Opened this website, so the whole menu can be browsed and ordered online for the first time.

Ordering something special?

For custom messages, dietary requirements, or a delivery date, place your order through the shop and then send the details through our contact page — a real person reads every message before your order goes into the oven.
